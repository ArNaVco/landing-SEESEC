FAQs

Which AWS certifications does SEESEC support?
We support all major AWS certifications including Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect (Associate & Professional), Developer, DevOps Engineer, Security Specialty, Data Analytics, and Machine Learning.
Can training be customized for our organization?
Absolutely. We design custom learning paths based on your team's roles, existing skills, and business objectives. We can also incorporate your internal use cases into workshops.
Are hands‑on labs included in the programs?
Yes, all programs include extensive hands-on labs using AWS Skill Builder, sandbox environments, and real-world case studies to ensure practical application of knowledge.
Do you provide executive and leadership workshops?
Yes, we offer executive briefings and leadership workshops focused on cloud strategy, governance, FinOps, and digital transformation to align leadership with technical teams.
Can training be delivered onsite, virtual, or hybrid?
All delivery modes are available. We can conduct onsite training at your premises, virtual instructor-led sessions, or hybrid models combining both for maximum flexibility.
