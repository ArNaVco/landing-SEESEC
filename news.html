Turning English Subtitles into Bulgarian, Automatically
The SEESEC Subtitle Generation Tool has been developed to automate one of the most repetitive parts of multilingual media localization.
 
The AI-powered solution takes an English subtitle file and converts it into a corresponding Bulgarian subtitle file, while preserving the subtitle structure and formatting required for subsequent video integration.

What would otherwise require significant manual translation and formatting effort can now be handled through a much faster automated workflow.
More importantly, this is not being looked at as a one-language utility. The underlying approach provides a foundation that can gradually be extended towards additional languages and larger automated media-localization workflows.
Another interesting build underway is our AI Accounting Automation Tool, designed around one of the most process-heavy functions inside most organizations: accounts payable.
The system is being developed to process invoices, compare them with corresponding Purchase Orders, validate payment terms, conduct GST and taxation checks, and support automated payment-approval workflows.
Instead of finance teams manually moving between invoices, POs, compliance checks, and approvals, the aim is to bring these activities into a unified intelligent workflow.
The expected outcome is straightforward: less repetitive verification, fewer avoidable errors, quicker processing, and better visibility across accounting operations.
This is exactly the type of automation we believe has the potential to make AI valuable inside an enterprise: not by adding another dashboard, but by removing hours of repetitive operational work
As the number and complexity of projects at SEESEC continues to grow, we are also building an Internal Project Management Tool designed specifically around the way our teams operate.
The platform is intended to create a single view across ongoing, completed, and upcoming projects, covering milestones, tasks, deadlines, dependencies, team allocation, and overall project status.
But we are taking it beyond internal task management.
The platform is also being designed to support project-level client communication, email sharing, deliverable tracking, team visibility, and collaboration, creating a centralized operational layer between our teams, management, and customers.
 
The larger objective is simple: as SEESEC scales, our internal operating systems should become smarter and more structured with us.

Making Recruitment More Intelligent
 
 
Hiring is another workflow we are actively automating internally.
Our HR Recruitment Portal is being developed as a centralized system for managing manpower requirements, candidates, interview schedules, interviewers, and recruitment stages.
The more interesting layer, however, is AI.
The platform will use AI-powered resume screening and candidate shortlisting, automatically comparing applicant profiles against defined job requirements to help HR teams identify relevant candidates faster.
We are also adding an AI Voice Bot for first-round candidate screening, capable of interacting with candidates in Hindi and English, conducting preliminary conversations, capturing responses, and supporting the evaluation process before candidates move forward.
The result we are working towards is a recruitment workflow that is faster, more structured, and substantially less dependent on repetitive manual coordination.
